Ended October and started November the slow way, in Elyu. 🌅

Sunset dinner by a clean, quiet shore with fresh buko, mango shake, and good food under string lights. Then barefoot nights at Flotsam & Jetsam with chill DJ sets, red-lit beers by the sea, and nowhere else to be.

And because Halloween wasn’t over yet, we ended up at Camper Bean watching spooky RC crawlers decked out in cobwebs, coffins, and fake blood. Tiny trucks, big effort, and so much fun. 🕸️🚙

Here’s to soft beginnings and more salty-air weekends. 🤍
