Honestly, I had a bit of downtime recently and decided to finally put it to good use. What started as just messing around with an idea ended up turning into a full-on new website!

I’ve been wanting to rebuild and refresh my space for a while now, so sitting down and actually bringing it all together felt really good. It’s definitely one of the bigger highlights of my latest life update.

Here’s a quick peek at what’s on the site right now:

- **Resume & Portfolio:** A central place to showcase my work and experience.
- **Easy Contact Channels:** Direct ways to reach out, send a message, or connect quickly.
- **Life Updates & Memories:** A dedicated space for personal updates and memories, beyond just social media.
- **More to Come:** I'll be adding new features whenever more downtime comes along!

I’d love for you to check it out and see what’s new. Hope you enjoy looking around, and I’ll catch you guys on the feed soon!
